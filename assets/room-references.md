# Room design references

- [Philips Hue SpatialAware](https://www.philips-hue.com/en-us/support/article/hue-spatialaware/000017) and [living room lighting](https://www.philips-hue.com/en-us/products/living-room-lighting) informed the placement and color of the floor lamps, task lighting, and TV wall wash. The room palettes are original interpretations.
- [Muuto In Situ](https://www.muuto.com/products/shop-by-family/in-situ-modular-sofa) provided reference imagery for seating arrangements, low upholstered profiles, and the space around the coffee table.
- The [Seattle Central Library reading rooms and Books Spiral](https://www.spl.org/hours-and-locations/central-library/central-library-highlights/central-library-tours) informed the library’s reading area and shelf display.
- [Amoeba Hollywood’s interiors](https://www.amoeba.com/our-stores/store-photos/hollywood/) informed the Record Store’s browsing bins and face-out sleeve display.
- [MIT MAD Making](https://mad.mit.edu/about/mad-making) informed the workshop’s task lighting, benches, and organized tool storage.
- [Apple’s visionOS materials guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials) informed the translucent, object-anchored panels.
- The rooms reuse [scanned furniture](curated/CREDITS.md), [surface scans](materials/CREDITS.md), and the portfolio’s existing album artwork. Prototype models were authored from Diego’s [project photos](projects/CREDITS.md). The TV and music use YouTube and Spotify’s official players.

- The expanded rooms use 12 additional [Poly Haven models](https://polyhaven.com/models), including the sofa, dining table, cassette player, bench vise, and articulated work lamp. The [model manifest](curated/room-models.json) lists their original sources and CC0 licenses. The apartment posters are original graphics inspired by music, soccer, and the Pacific Northwest.
